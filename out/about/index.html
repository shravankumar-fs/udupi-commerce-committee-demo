About the Chamber

Udupi's chamber of commerce, since 2003

The Udupi Chamber of Commerce and Industry (UCCI) brings together traders, manufacturers and service businesses of Udupi district — like the chambers of commerce in Mumbai or Bengaluru, rooted in our coastal town.

Who we are

UCCI was registered on 5 February 2003 as a not-for-profit company limited by guarantee. It works from Chamber Tower on Railway Godown Road, Indrali, and is led by an elected President, office bearers and a board of directors who serve one-year terms.

What we do

Represent members

Take the concerns of traders and industries — tariffs, taxes, infrastructure — to the district administration and the state government.

Grow local trade & tourism

Seminars and partnerships with hotel, homestay and trade associations to grow Udupi's economy.

Keep members informed

Share circulars, tax changes and government schemes quickly and clearly.

Connect businesses

Bring together businesses across sectors — from Malpe's fisheries to Manipal's services.
