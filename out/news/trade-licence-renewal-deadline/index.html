Circular3 Oct 2026

Trade licence renewal: last date 31 October

Udupi City Municipal Council has notified 31 October 2026 as the last date for trade licence renewal for the year 2026–27 without penalty.

Members who need help with the online application can visit the committee office between 10 am and 5 pm with their previous licence copy and property tax receipt.
